## Goal

Show whether the own sender retransmits spuriously or the bound (own <= 2 x kernel + 8) is ill-founded; 100 runs pass.

## Done and verified

- Reproduced under CUBIC: own 31 to 51 segments sent again in 5 of 60 runs (bound about 22), typical 5 to 7, kernel 7 to 9.
- Not spurious: in a bad run the router tbf dropped 41 packets and the own sender sent 43 again. They are real losses from a slow start that overshoots the 22 packet queue.
- Cause: `Inner::pump` read the clock once and looped passes with that time. A pass takes milliseconds in a debug build, so segments and their ACKs got the same instant, round trip samples read 1 us, and HyStart's smallest round trip became 1 us. With the ACK train broken by a gap over 2 ms, the exit came at cwnd 40 instead of 17. Fix: read the clock before each step (engine/fps-tcp-own/src/lib.rs). Test `a_pump_pass_reads_the_clock_before_each_of_its_steps` fails without it.
- The TRN-34 baseline row pinned to CUBIC (the sandbox default BBR gives 100 to 140 there).
- After the fix, 485 row pairs under CUBIC: 2 outliers above 22 (131, and 27 found by trace).

## Remaining

Residual heavy tail of about 0.5 to 1 percent per run (own 92 vs kernel 25 in a full run, 131 in another). The kernel baseline has the same tail (24 to 38 in about 3 percent of runs). One outlier was traced: a 2.6 ms gap between ACK batches ended the HyStart ACK train late. The cause of the 131 case is not found.
