## Goal

With options on, the own TCP sender reaches at least 80 percent of its options-off goodput through a 20 Mbit/s, 32 KiB tail-drop queue, with at most twice the kernel client's retransmissions.

## Done and verified

- Reproduction: `cargo test -p fps-tcp --test bottleneck` on the code of main: options off 18.75 Mbit/s with 41 retransmissions, options on 5.31 Mbit/s with 746 retransmissions and 7 timeouts. The test fails.
- Matrix (own to own, same link): each option alone, and SACK with NewReno, run at 18 to 19 Mbit/s. SACK with CUBIC and window scaling collapses (1.4 to 4.8 Mbit/s, 9 to 10 timeouts, 1000 to 1500 retransmissions).
- Trace: the first SACK recovery ran with a window of 47 KB (above the queue), its retransmission was dropped by the full queue, nothing after it was sent as the flight grew to 535 KB (the pipe counted the lost copy), the timer fired, and slow start restarted at 0.7 of the grown flight.
- Fix: lost retransmission detection in the scoreboard (`sack.rs`, `host.rs`), CUBIC reduces from `min(cwnd, flight)` and a timeout in a recovery keeps the threshold (`cubic.rs`, `recovery.rs`).
- After: test passes (18.75 against 18.63 Mbit/s, 48 against 37 retransmissions, 0 timeouts). `cargo test -p fps-tcp-own -p fps-tcp` passes, clippy `-D warnings --all-targets`, `cargo fmt --check`, `cargo deny check`, `plan.py check`, `render --check`, `lint` are clean.
- Kernel row in the sandbox (root, no netem): own client to kernel server, bottleneck: 19.2 Mbit/s with 43 to 67 retransmissions, from 7.4 with 192 to 254.

## Decisions taken in this package

- The own to smoltcp pairing is not in the regression test: smoltcp 0.14 keeps four out of order ranges and drops the rest without a SACK block, so the own sender stalls on two timeouts on this link whatever the cause above. Reported as a finding.
- The retransmission bound of the test is `2 * (options off) + 40`, because the own stack has no pacing or HyStart and sends 40 to 70 segments again where the kernel sends 9. The bound sits well below the collapse (746 to 1500).
