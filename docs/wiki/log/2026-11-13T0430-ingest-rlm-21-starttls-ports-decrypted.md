## [2026-11-13] ingest | RLM-21 STARTTLS ports in the decrypted view

`python3 -m realism decrypted` gained `--starttls-port PORT:PROTOCOL` (`smtp`, `imap`, or `pop`, repeatable). Each tshark run of the command (ClientHello list, payload list of `--plaintext upgrade`, `follow,tls`) passes `-d tcp.port==P,PROTOCOL` for it, and tshark's SMTP, IMAP, and POP3 dissectors then switch the session to TLS after the STARTTLS reply. `Tshark.run`, `read_sessions`, and `decrypted.run` take `starttls_ports`. RLM-18 could not do this from the labels, because with `--view-port` they name the port of the view (587, 143) and not of the capture (5587, 1143), and decoding a STARTTLS port as TLS from byte 0 feeds the plain greeting to the TLS dissector. A port in both the option and the labels is decoded as the protocol.

Evidence: with real tshark 4.2.2, three STARTTLS sessions on 5587 (smtp) and on 1143 (imap), with the ClientHello whole, cut after 1 octet, and cut after 4: plain tshark and `-d ...,tls` list 0 to 2 of 3, `-d ...,smtp` or `,imap` lists 3. `realism/tests/test_decrypted.py` asserts this for all six cases, checks the arguments of each run with a stand-in tshark, the option parser, the refusal of a malformed option, and runs the command on the split smtp capture (fails without the option, passes with it and shows the upgrade view). `realism/tests/tlsgen.py` `first_segment` now cuts the ClientHello and no longer the first client flight, so it works for STARTTLS dialogues. The smtp and imap module workflows (`mod-smtp.yml`, `mod-imap.yml`) and `test_decrypted_fixtures.py` pass `--starttls-port 5587:smtp` and `--starttls-port 1143:imap`. The report format stays 0.17.0: no field changed, so the controller schema copy and `ui/src/api/schema.d.ts` are untouched.

Not changed (outside the paths): `modules/smtp/fixtures/capture.sh`, `modules/imap/fixtures/capture.sh`, and `modules/smtp/tests/fixture.rs`, `modules/imap/tests/fixture.rs` call `decrypted` without the option and keep the heuristic. They still work; a MOD follow up can add the flag.
