## [2026-11-10] ingest | RLM-08 Session structure check

We added the `session_structure` check to the realism report. The runner reads the flow graph of a scenario file (flows with their transport and server port, `after` edges, and `spawn` edges with their loop and branch counts), groups the sessions of the capture by client address into scenario instances, and compares the connection count and the order with the graph. A flow occurs as often as its parent spawns it, an `after` edge requires the later flow to start once the earlier flow completed, and a `spawn` edge requires the child to start while its parent runs. A failure names the scenario, the instance, and the violated edge with both sessions and their times. The check is opt in, through a `structure` section in the labels or `--scenario FILE`, and the report format moved to 0.6.0. The evidence is `docs/handoff/RLM-08.md` and `realism/tests/test_structure.py`, where the `web_browse` scenario of the specification (a lookup, then the page) passes and a capture with the page fetch before the lookup fails on the edge `after lookup -> page`, and where the `mixed_session` scenario of `modules/ftp` checks one control and three data connections. We described the check, its grouping rule, and its limits in [Realism](Realism.md#session-structure-check).
