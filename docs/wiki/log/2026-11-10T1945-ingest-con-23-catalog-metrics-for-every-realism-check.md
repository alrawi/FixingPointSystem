## [2026-11-10] ingest | CON-23 Catalog metrics for every realism check

We closed the gap that CON-22 found: of the eight check kinds of the realism report only `conformance` and `temporal` (and the classifier series of `mix_accuracy`) carried metrics, so a failing `parser_errors`, `encryption`, `content`, `session_structure`, or `transport_behavior` check added no `RealismResult`. The owner approved the change on 2026-11-10 and chose catalog metrics per check kind. Metric catalog 0.5.0 adds seven gauges: `realism.check_failures` (every check carries one, 0 exactly when the check passed), `realism.parser_errors`, `realism.decrypted_ratio`, `realism.ja4_match_ratio`, `realism.duplicate_ratio`, `realism.structure_failures`, and `realism.transport_deviations`. The new labels are `check` and `parser`, and `property` gains the transport fields. The entry `contracts/changes/CON-23.md` extends the mapping of CON-22 with a target and a rule for `passed` for every metric (the `mix.` series included), and `contracts/examples/realism/check-metrics.yaml` holds a passing and a failing case for each kind. The report schema lives under `realism/`, so the entry holds its exact change as a patch for the runner package. We updated [Contracts](../Contracts.md) and `contracts/README.md`.
