## [2026-11-18] ingest | TRN-23 loss recovery and NewReno in the own stack

TRN-23 gave the own stack an RTT estimator with the RFC 6298 timeout, fast retransmit, an out of order receive queue, and NewReno, without a per socket cost for idle connections.

- `fps-tcp-own/src/recovery.rs` holds the pure parts with unit tests: `RttEstimator` (SRTT, RTTVAR, RTO between a minimum of 200 ms and 2 s, backoff), `is_dupack`, `NewReno` (slow start by acknowledged bytes, congestion avoidance, fast retransmit on the third duplicate with the `recover` guard of RFC 6582, inflation, partial and full acknowledgments, Limited Transmit, timeout), `OooQueue` (non overlapping runs inside the window, merge, drain, a held FIN), and `Recovery`, the state of one connection (Karn's probe, `snd_max`, the retransmit flag).
- `host.rs` uses them. A connection holds `Option<Box<Recovery>>`, made when data is sent or held behind a hole and dropped when nothing is in flight, nothing waits to be sent, and nothing is held (8 bytes idle). The sender keeps `snd_nxt` going back after a timeout (go back N) with `snd_max` in the box, so a cumulative acknowledgment that jumps over the data the receiver held out of order is accepted. The receiver sends an ACK at once for every out of order segment. The receiver sends a window update when a read opened the window by a segment or half the buffer since the last advertised window (`adv_wnd`, 2 bytes), which the congestion window made necessary: the old rule fired only on a nearly closed window and halved the 0 percent goodput.
- `OwnConfig` has `min_rto_us` (200 ms) and `initial_cwnd_segments` (10). `HostCounters` has `timeouts`, `fast_retransmits`, and `ooo_segments`; `retransmits` counts every segment sent again.
- Results are in `engine/fps-tcp/results/sandbox-2vcpu-trn23/` with `host.txt`. The own stack at 20 ms per direction: 12.0 Mbit/s at 0 percent, 7.5 at 0.1 percent (3 of 3), and 3.12 at 1 percent (3 of 3; it was 12.2, 8.0, and 1.8 with one transfer unfinished). At 8 Mbit/s with a 64 KiB queue: 7.69 Mbit/s with 1 retransmission per transfer (was 1.32 with 706). The 1,000,000 socket row: 261 and 262 B per socket against 253 and 255 for main in the same session (+8 B, the `None` of the box), and a probe rate within 4 percent of main (47.8k and 48.8k per second against 49.9k and 48.9k). The kernel row at loss and delay is not measured in the sandbox (no `sch_netem`), so the 50 percent acceptance is checked by the coordinator on the hosted runner against the kernel's 4.2 Mbit/s (threshold 2.1).

Tests: 30 unit tests of the mechanisms in `recovery.rs` (RTO computation, Karn, the duplicate rule, NewReno transitions, the queue including the sequence wrap), 14 host level tests in `host/recovery_tests.rs` (two hosts driven by hand with a set clock: fast retransmit without the timer, window halving, a partial acknowledgment, a lost tail and the timeout, Karn with exact timeouts, a FIN behind a hole, state only in flight, the window bound of the queue, the size of a connection), and two in `tests/lossy.rs` (1 percent loss with 20 ms, reordering).
