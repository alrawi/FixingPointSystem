## [2026-11-12] ingest | RLM-15 Packet inter arrival times inside a session

The temporal check of the realism runner has a third property, `packet_gap` (`realism/temporal.py`, report format 0.15.0): the gaps between consecutive packets with payload inside one direction of a session, per application, against a target of a new `temporal.packets` labels section. The constant of that section is paced media: `ms` is one packet time or a list (the sip_rtp fixture has calls of 20 ms and 30 ms), `tolerance_ms` is required, and `within` (default 0.9) is the share of the gaps of each stream that must be on time. Each stream with at least `min_samples` gaps is judged alone, so a call with jittered packets fails among good ones, and the reason names the worst stream, for example `packet gaps of application rtp: 2 of 4 streams are not paced at 20 ms, because more than 10.0% of their gaps are further than 5 ms from the packet time; the worst is the udp stream ... (session 2, client side), where 39.4% of 99 gaps are further than 5 ms from 20 ms`. The other distributions run the KS test on the gaps of all streams together. The evidence is `docs/handoff/RLM-15.md` and `realism/tests/test_packet_gaps.py` (36 tests, one of them on the committed sip_rtp fixture, which passes with `ms: [20, 30]`). We described it in [Realism](Realism.md#temporal-distribution-check) and in `realism/README.md`.

Decision: packet gaps add no `realism.ks_*` series, because the property label of those metrics in the catalog 0.6.0 has no value for them. Only `realism.check_failures` reports them.
