## [2026-11-28] ingest | bug #689: the own stack keeps a connection that was reset before accept

- In `engine/fps-tcp-own`, a client that connects and resets at once sends `RST|ACK` before its plain ACK of the SYN-ACK. The server still held the connection in SYN-RECEIVED and closed it without queueing it, so `accept` timed out (`strike/conn_churn`: 18 of 24 sessions failed on AF_XDP).
- `on_segment` in `host.rs` now completes the handshake when a reset acknowledges our SYN-ACK: the connection joins the accept queue of its listener, `accept` returns it, and the first read fails with `ConnectionReset`, as on a kernel. A reset without a valid ACK still drops a half open connection.
- The first CI run of `modules (strike)` still failed 6 of 24 sessions (`unexpected_reset` on the client side, `connect_timeout` on the server side). Cause: `connected` returned the reset as the error of `connect` when the server accepted and reset before the connecting task looked at its socket; a kernel's `connect` has succeeded at the SYN-ACK. A new `handshook` flag of the connection (set when the SYN-ACK arrives) makes `connect` succeed, and the first read or write fails with the reset. The datapath test `strike_conn_churn_runs_through_the_agent_on_the_datapath_without_a_failed_session` (`engine/fps-agent/tests/datapath_run.rs`, in memory, no root) runs the scenario through the agent: 16 of 47 sessions failed before the second fix, none after, and it fails 21 of 41 sessions on `main`.
- `strike/conn_churn` left the `excluded` list of `profiles.yaml` (the `excluded` key and the `drop_rules` of strike are gone, so its labels are scored again) and the pinned list in `engine/fps-agent/src/backend.rs`, which is now empty. Regression tests: four in `engine/fps-tcp-own/tests/own.rs` (one connection, one whose plain ACK is lost, 100 of them, and a connect that is reset before the task looks) and the agent test below.
- The structure check of the first CI run (`session_structure`, 3 of 25 conn_churn instances) failed because the first instance dialed its churned children on 8082 before any spawned server flow listened there, the own stack answered RST, and the runtime's retry of a refused connect made a 33rd churn connection. The agent now listens on the constant accept ports of the spawned TCP flows when the AF_XDP transport starts (`Assembly::spawned_tcp_ports` in `assemble.rs`, `DatapathTransport::prelisten_tcp` in `transport.rs`, called from `Prepared::start_on`); the hub parks an early session for `PENDING_GRACE` (bug #688) until the flow subscribes. Pinned by `a_port_that_is_listened_on_ahead_accepts_a_dial_that_comes_before_any_call` in `engine/fps-agent/tests/datapath_transport.rs` (the connect is refused before `prelisten_tcp`, and completes after). A spawned flow whose port is drawn at run time (the ftp data ports) is not pre-listened and keeps the lazy bind.
- `profiles.yaml`: the leftover `drop_rules` of dns (tcp 53, only `tcp_fallback` is TCP) and `drop_applications` of hls (`hls_playlist`, `hls_segment` on 8091 and 8092, only `vod_segments` uses them) are gone, so those labels are scored again. `test_finalize.py` follows.
