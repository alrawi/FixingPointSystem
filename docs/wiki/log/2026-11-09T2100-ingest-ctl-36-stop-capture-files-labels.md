## [2026-11-09] ingest | CTL-36 stopCapture, capture files, and labeled criteria

The controller serves `POST /v1/runs/{id}/captures/{capture_id}:stop` (`captures.Service.Stop`, `captures.API.StopCapture`) and fills `Capture.files` from `captures.AgentFile` in every capture answer. `export_tls_keys` of the REST request reaches `StartCaptureCommand`. The compiler copies the `where` filter of a criterion into `Criterion.labels` and refuses a plan when an agent reports an engine_api below 0.2.3 (`compiler.ErrAgentTooOld`, fed by one line in the run orchestrator), and `agents.Manager.Prepare` refuses to send such a plan (`agents.ErrPlanUnsupported`). `stopCapture` left `pendingOps` of the wiring test, and `pending.StopCapture` forwards to the `CaptureHandler`. The flake in `TestDownloadResumesAfterAnInterruptedUpload` was a race in the fake agent, which cancelled the upload call after a 50 ms sleep and could discard chunks that the controller had not read, so the controller resumed below the offset that the test computed from the bytes that the fake agent counted. The fake agent now half closes the call, which keeps the order of the stream. See [Controller](../Controller.md#captures).
