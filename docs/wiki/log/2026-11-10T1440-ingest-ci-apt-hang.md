## [2026-11-10] ingest | CI apt hang: bounded apt calls and job timeouts

Between 05:14 and 06:07 UTC on 2026-11-10, 14 runs on runner image `ubuntu-24.04` 20261101.360.1 hung for exactly 360 minutes each, until GitHub's six hour limit cancelled the job: seven realism runs (`module (pop3)` and `module (dns)`), three agt runs (`standalone-netns`), one trn (`capture`), one rlm (`test`), one mod-http1 and one mod-smtp. In every stuck job `sudo apt-get update -q` printed `Ign:` for each `http://azure.archive.ubuntu.com` entry of `/etc/apt/apt-mirrors.txt`, fell back to `https://archive.ubuntu.com`, fetched the `noble-updates`, `noble-backports` and `noble-security` InRelease files, and then wrote nothing more. The agt job installs only `iproute2 ethtool`, so the Zeek repository is not the cause. apt has no overall time limit and none of the jobs set `timeout-minutes`. The runner-images tracker has the same failover freeze as [actions/runner-images#15040](https://github.com/actions/runner-images/issues/15040), with a 2026-10-28 report of jobs that hit the six hour cap after the same `Get:5 ... noble-security InRelease` line, and the slow Azure mirror as [#14823](https://github.com/actions/runner-images/issues/14823). Successful runs of the same window also spent longer in apt than usual, up to 205 s for `apt-get update` plus `tshark`.

`tools/ci/apt-install.sh` now wraps every apt call of a hosted runner. It writes `Acquire::Retries "3"` and 20 s `http`, `https` and `ftp` acquire timeouts to `/etc/apt/apt.conf.d/zzz-fps-ci` (the name sorts after the image's own `zz-retries`, which would otherwise win), runs `apt-get update` under `timeout` 300 s with three attempts, and `apt-get install -y -q --no-install-recommends` under 600 s with three attempts. After a failed or stopped update it rewrites `/etc/apt/sources.list.d/ubuntu.sources` so that the stanzas use `https://archive.ubuntu.com/ubuntu` and, for `noble-security`, `https://security.ubuntu.com/ubuntu` instead of the mirror list, with the components and `Signed-By` unchanged. A failed install applies the same fallback once and refreshes the lists. Options before the package names go to `apt-get install`, and `--install-recommends` keeps recommends for the steps that installed with them. The workflows agt, cap, eng-capture, hst, image, mod-dns, mod-http1, mod-http2, mod-tls, qa-gate1, qa-harness, release and trn call it, and `realism/ci/install-tools.sh` and `realism/ci/build-ndpi.sh` call it for the module, realism, rlm and gate jobs. `tools/ci/test_apt_install.sh` tests the helper without root or network through stubs of `apt-get`, `dpkg` and `sudo`, and the `scripts` job of `ci.yml` runs it. Every job on a hosted runner has a `timeout-minutes` of about three times its slowest success of the last 20 runs, rounded up to 5 minutes, between 15 and 60; the KVM job of `image.yml` keeps 120.
