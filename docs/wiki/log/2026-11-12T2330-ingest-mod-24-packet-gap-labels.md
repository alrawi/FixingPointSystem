## [2026-11-12] ingest | MOD-24 Packet gap labels in place of the module timing scripts

The sip_rtp fixture now checks its RTP packet interval through the `temporal.packets` labels of RLM-15, and `modules/sip_rtp/fixtures/rtp_check.py` with its tests is gone. `expected.yaml` has `rtp: {dist: constant, ms: [20, 30], tolerance_ms: 8, within: 0.9}`, and the existing `python -m realism score` of the module job and of `capture.sh` judges it, so the workflow lost its separate step and `mk/mod-sip.mk` its call. `fixtures/test_packet_labels.py` (4 tests, pure Python, no tshark) shows that the committed capture passes (24 streams judged, none failed) and that the same capture fails with `not paced at` when the packets of its busiest media flow are shifted by up to 14 ms, and passes with 1 ms. The http2 wrapper `fixtures/tshark-tls-ports.sh`, its Rust test `tests/tshark_wrapper.rs` (and the `[[test]]` entry), and `tests/split_client_hello.py` are gone, because RLM-18 decodes the labelled TLS ports in the runner: `python3 -m realism decrypted` lists 40 of 40 TLS sessions of the committed capture without the wrapper (run here with tshark 4.2.2), and `realism/tests/test_decrypted.py` keeps the split ClientHello case.

Decision: hls keeps `hls_check.py`, and gets no packet gap labels. We tried `temporal.packets` with `direction: client` on `hls_vod` and `hls_live` (constant 400 ms, tolerance 40 ms): the client stream of a keepalive connection alternates playlist requests and segment requests, so 33% (vod_ladder, 12 gaps) to 56% (live_refresh, 16 gaps) of the gaps are 0.2 to 0.4 ms and the check fails, and a playback has 8 to 16 gaps against the 30 that a stream needs, so the `min_samples` of the whole temporal section would have to drop to 8. The labels also read no bytes, and `hls_check.py` carries the byte share and bit rate acceptance of MOD-15 (5%). The segment cadence of `vod_segments` is already an `arrivals` label. Removing the script would drop these checks, so the pull request leaves it and the coordinator decides.

Module-Development.md gained a paragraph in Fixtures and realism that tells authors to write pacing as a `temporal.packets` target, with the sip_rtp example, the `min_samples` limit, what the labels cannot see, and the advice to test the labels with a jittered capture. The `temporal` row of its table now says "targets", because a constant is not a KS test. Modules.md and the READMEs of sip_rtp and http2 follow.

Looser than the script: the labels list the packet times (a scenario with another `ptime_ms` needs an entry in `ms`), and they test no mean gap, so a stream that drifts by a constant 1.5 ms passes with `tolerance_ms: 8`. The exact pacing stays with the paused clock tests of `tests/scenarios.rs`.
