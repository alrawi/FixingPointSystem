## [2026-11-11] ingest | RLM-17 The CON-23 metrics in the realism report

The realism report is at format 0.12.0, and every check of the eight kinds that the metric catalog 0.5.0 names in its label `check` carries `realism.check_failures` and the series of its kind: `realism.conformance_ratio`, `realism.ks_statistic` and `realism.ks_p_value`, `realism.parser_errors` (per application and parser, and with the application `all` per parser), `realism.decrypted_ratio` and `realism.ja4_match_ratio`, `realism.duplicate_ratio`, `realism.structure_failures`, the `mix.*` series with `source` `classifier`, and `realism.transport_deviations`. The schema requires the failures series in each of them (exactly one, with the label of its kind, 0 exactly when `passed` is true), `transport.py` returns its series, the `#89` TODOs are gone, and `realism/series.py` builds the failures series. `premature_connection_reuse` joined `ZEEK_STATE_WEIRDS` in `realism/parsers.py`.

The schema change is `contracts/changes/CON-23-realism-report-schema.patch` applied by hand against the 0.11.0 schema (one hunk did not apply: RLM-14 added the `messages` entry to `$defs.check.allOf`). The two generic rules of the patch (one failures series per check, and its value follows `passed`) are wrapped in an `if` on the eight kinds, so they do not apply to `messages`.

Decision on `messages`: the catalog (0.5.0) has no value `messages` for the label `check`, so `realism.check_failures{check=messages}` would break the catalog. The messages check keeps `metrics` empty (`maxItems: 0` in the schema) and is outside the failures rule. A failing `messages` check shows in the report and the exit code, not in `RunReport.realism`. The follow up is a CON change: add `messages` to the values of the label `check` (catalog 0.6.0, plus the mirror in `engine/fps-stats/src/catalog.rs` `LABEL_VALUES`, the controller copy of the catalog, and `contracts/examples/realism/check-metrics.yaml` with a passing and a failing `messages` case), and say how `realism.check_failures` counts for it (`failures_total`, which is `len(failures)`). Then an RLM change removes `messages` from the exclusion in the schema, emits the series in `realism/messages.py`, and takes it out of `CATALOG_CHECKS` in `realism/series.py`.

Findings: a check that fails without a reason (`mix_accuracy` with no basis to judge) would show 0 failures, so the series is `max(count, 1)` for a failed check, as the schema requires. `premature_connection_reuse` is added to the ignore set unconditionally, not only for sessions with more than one connection: Zeek raises it only for a reused 5-tuple, which is the case of more than one connection in one session, and the parser layer knows no connection counts. The Gate 1 rule of QA-09 (`REUSE_WEIRDS` with `reused_tuple`) is stricter and stays until the owner of the suite drops it. Zeek is not installed in the sandbox, so the weird is tested on a literal `weird.log`.
