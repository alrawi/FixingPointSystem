## [2026-11-12] ingest | RLM-18 TLS on non standard ports in the decrypted check

`realism/decrypted.py` listed the TLS sessions of a capture with plain tshark, which reads a port other than 443 as TLS only by its heuristic. MOD-23 reproduced a split ClientHello (first segment of 1 to 4 octets) on port 8443 that the heuristic missed, which gave "39 TLS sessions, expected 40" in the http2 module job. The fix is in the runner: `read_sessions(..., tls_ports)` and `Tshark.run(..., tls_ports)` pass `-d tcp.port==P,tls` for every port (443 excepted) on each tshark run (the ClientHello list, the payload list of `--plaintext upgrade`, and the `follow,tls` run), and `run` takes the ports from the TCP `server_port` of the session rules of the labels (`labelled_tls_ports`). The encryption check already did the same.

Evidence: `realism/tests/test_decrypted.py` has a test of the port list, a test with a stand-in tshark that records the arguments of each run, a test that `--view-port` adds none, and, with real tshark and openssl, a capture of three sessions on port 8443 whose ClientHello is cut after 1 and after 4 octets: `read_sessions` without ports lists none, with the port it lists all three, and the command passes with `--sessions 3`. `realism/tests/tlsgen.py` gained `first_segment` for that. The report format stays 0.17.0, because no field changed.

Decisions: with `--view-port` the labels name the port of the view, not of the capture, so no port is added and the smtp (587, STARTTLS, capture port 5587) and imap (143) captures keep the heuristic. Decoding their STARTTLS ports as TLS from the first byte would also feed the plain start of the dialogue to the TLS dissector. They need an explicit option for the capture ports if they ever show the same miss, and no such option exists yet. The wrapper `modules/http2/fixtures/tshark-tls-ports.sh` stays for MOD-24, and http1 (ports 8443 to 8446) gets the fix without a change in the module.
